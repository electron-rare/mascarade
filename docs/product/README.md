## Current Product Thesis

The next maturity step is not surface expansion.

The product now needs a stable contract:

- `ops` shows real state and triggers action.
- `Mascarade` helps decide without weakening trust.
- `Kill_LIFE` materializes execution continuity over time.

The system becomes more credible when one situation can travel across the three surfaces without losing identity, context, or traceability.
